The rework, in frames

Calmer pages, same look

Six pages reworked in the existing language: League Gothic, Jost, the black plate, the waterline, teal rationed. Phone first at 390, then desktop at 1440 as its own composition. Nothing that worked was removed; things were grouped, folded or moved onto the plate.

What was cut

  • Section numbers on things that are not steps; step numbers stay as the dashes.
  • Helper sentences under controls, all of them. A label or a segment word says it.
  • Chip rows that wrap: the namer, the competition status, the gear kinds.
  • Three-fact metadata lines. One dot, or two lines.
  • "Not measured" on rows. A size not taken is not there.
  • The like and comment count sentence under the icons.
  • The second log form as a separate page.

What moved or folded

  • Feed, competition tabs and Start one: onto the black plate.
  • Follow: up to the card header, beside the angler.
  • How many, depth, water, competition entry: under More in the log.
  • Gear by kind with search: inside the Gear picker's sheet.
  • Saved spot and new spot: behind Not this spot and Add as a spot.
  • Conditions: three League Gothic readouts, the hour in the label, Refresh beside it.
  • The competition clock and its two actions: onto the plate.

Components to map

  • PageHead gains a children slot for tabs, buttons and the clock.
  • PhotoBlock owns the frame, Change and Remove, the strip, and hosts SpeciesGuess as its black band.
  • MeasureField, Segment, Picker, Fold unchanged in kind; Picker rows take a dashed underline.
  • MapLocationPicker keeps search, locate and base on the map; one source line under it.
  • Readouts from the home page, reused for Conditions.
  • CompetitionBanner and CompetitionEntryFields as black block and tape cell.
  • New: RulesTable, EntryRow with CheckLine, PublishPreview on step 3 of the log.
